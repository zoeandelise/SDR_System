在数据库中执行以下SQL验证：

```sql
-- 查看饮食记录示例
SELECT 
    dr.record_id,
    u.nick_name,
    dr.meal_type,
    dr.notes,
    dr.total_calories,
    dr.record_date
FROM diet_record dr
LEFT JOIN sys_user u ON dr.user_id = u.user_id
WHERE dr.user_id >= 101
ORDER BY dr.record_date DESC, dr.user_id
LIMIT 20;
```
