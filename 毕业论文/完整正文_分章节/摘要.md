# 摘  要

随着慢性代谢类疾病的持续攀升和居民膳食结构的快速变迁，饮食健康管理正面临着一些核心挑战，例如营养数据呈现出碎片化的状态，个性化推荐的准确性有待提升，疾病敏感成分的过滤机制也存在一定的粗糙性，很难支撑在多病种约束场景下进行成分级别的精细化安全校验以及开展差异化的膳食指导工作，行业迫切需要一种新型的解决方案，这种方案要把智能推荐技术和健康风险管控融合起来，借助这样的方式来应对用户个体健康差异以及饮食需求日益多样化的趋势。

本文设计并且实现了一个基于SpringBoot的智能饮食推荐系统，这个系统依靠RuoYi快速开发框架来构建轻量化的技术架构，选择了IntelliJ IDEA作为集成开发环境，把MySQL当作核心的数据存储引擎，基于B/S架构实现了系统的前后端分离部署，构建了一个普通用户和管理员双角色协同的功能体系，这个体系能够支持健康档案管理、饮食记录追踪、营养数据分析以及智能膳食推荐等多维度的业务功能。最终实现了一个智能饮食管理平台，这个平台把疾病敏感成分硬过滤、协同过滤个性化推荐以及可视化营养分析这些功能整合在了一起。

系统的设计涉及了从"健康建档 — 饮食记录 — 营养分析 — 智能推荐"的全流程数字化模块，借助运用基础代谢率精算与疾病标签匹配技术，实现了对糖尿病、高血压、痛风、高血脂等多种慢性病的成分级别安全过滤，并且在推荐算法设计时采用了分层架构的方式，这样系统就可以在安全过滤的基础上叠加用户协同过滤算法，从而实现安全性与个性化的有机平衡。凭借对饮食管理智能化升级路径的探索，为个人健康管理领域的数字化转型提供了一个可以借鉴的实践案例，这个案例有一定的理论研究价值，也有实际的应用意义。

**关键词：** 智能饮食推荐；协同过滤算法；SpringBoot框架；疾病安全过滤；营养分析







# ABSTRACT

With the continuous rise of chronic metabolic diseases and the rapid transformation of dietary structures, diet health management is facing core challenges such as fragmented nutritional data, low accuracy of personalized recommendations, and rough filtering mechanisms for disease-sensitive components, which hinder component-level refined safety verification and differentiated dietary guidance under multi-disease constraint scenarios. The industry urgently needs innovative solutions integrating intelligent recommendation technologies with health risk control to address individual health differences and increasingly diversified dietary demands.

This paper designs and implements an intelligent diet recommendation system based on SpringBoot. The system adopts a lightweight technical architecture built on the RuoYi rapid development framework, uses IntelliJ IDEA as the integrated development environment, employs MySQL as the core data storage engine, and achieves front-end and back-end separated deployment based on the B/S architecture. It constructs a dual-role functional system for regular users and administrators, supporting multidimensional business functions including health profile management, diet record tracking, nutritional data analysis, and intelligent meal recommendations. Ultimately, an intelligent diet management platform is realized, integrating disease-sensitive component hard filtering, collaborative filtering personalized recommendations, and visual nutritional analysis.

The system's design covers end-to-end digital modules spanning "health profiling — diet recording — nutritional analysis — intelligent recommendation." Through basal metabolic rate calculation and disease tag matching technologies, it enables component-level safety filtering for multiple chronic diseases including diabetes, hypertension, gout, and hyperlipidemia. Additionally, the recommendation algorithm design incorporates a layered architecture to overlay user collaborative filtering algorithms on the foundation of safety filtering, thereby achieving an organic balance between safety and personalization. This exploration of intelligent upgrading paths for diet management provides a replicable practical case for the digital transformation of personal health management, offering both theoretical research value and practical application significance.

**KEYWORDS:** Intelligent diet recommendation; Collaborative filtering algorithm; SpringBoot framework; Disease safety filtering; Nutritional analysis
